# Danhassan & Co., Inc. - Corporate Overview

## Introduction

Danhassan & Co., Inc. is a multinational holding investment conglomerate headquartered in Abuja, Nigeria. As one of Africa’s most diversified and influential investment firms, Danhassan & Co. is committed to shaping the future of energy, infrastructure, and digital innovation across the continent. With a focus on patient capital, operational excellence, and bold innovation, the company is driving Africa’s industrial renaissance by building resilient economies, enabling job creation, and fostering sustainable growth for generations to come.

The firm operates as a diversified conglomerate with a portfolio spanning power & utilities, infrastructure, real estate, finance, energy, media, and technology. Danhassan & Co. invests in core infrastructure essential for economic development, powering homes and industries, connecting cities and communities, financing homes and commercial growth, manufacturing materials that build nations, driving the continent’s digital future, and informing the world through media.

**Key Headquarters:**

- Primary: Abuja, Nigeria
- Notable Facility: SouthBlue Towers, Casablanca (Pacific Gas Company headquarters)

**Founders:**

- Anwar Alhassan: Co-founder, Chairman & CEO
- Husseini Mujeli: Co-founder, President & Board Member

## History and Milestones

Danhassan & Co. has a rich history of strategic growth spanning over three decades, evolving from energy distribution to a powerhouse in Africa's infrastructure landscape.

- **1993:** Anwar Alhassan & Husseini Mujeli co-found Frontier Petroleum Ltd, focusing on oil retail and distribution.
- **1998:** Sale of Frontier Petroleum completed. Danhassan & Co., Inc. is founded with a vision to build Africa’s infrastructure future.
- **2005:** Launch of South Atlantic Energy Plc – marking a pivotal entry into power generation.
- **2012:** Expansion into real estate & mortgage finance through the establishment of Lighthouse Finance & REIT.
- **2019:** Strategic acquisition of Shinrai LLC, positioning Danhassan in the high-growth data center industry.
- **2024:** Achieved record revenues and expanded commitment to sustainable and digital infrastructure projects across the continent.

Over 25 years, Danhassan & Co. has transitioned from foundational energy operations to a diversified leader in Africa's core infrastructure sectors.

## Business Segments and Portfolio

Danhassan & Co.'s portfolio is strategically diversified across critical sectors, with majority stakes in leading operating companies. The firm invests in platforms that power progress, build futures, connect the continent, and fuel industry.

### 1. Power & Electric Utility

- **Description:** Generating, transmitting, and distributing power across millions of homes and businesses.
- **Key Companies:**
  - South Atlantic Energy Plc (SAE): Power generation.
  - SATCo Ltd: Transmission.
  - Kano Electric: Distribution, serving 4.2M customers nationwide.
- **Impact:** Supplies 28% of Nigeria's national grid.
- **Focus Areas:** Renewable energy (solar, wind), power plants, transmission lines.

### 2. Construction & Materials

- **Description:** Supplying the materials that build Africa’s future, including steel, cement, aggregates, and asphalt for major infrastructure projects and regional transport corridors.
- **Key Company:** Atlas Materials Plc.
- **Focus Areas:** Manufacturing for construction, industrial materials.

### 3. Data Center & Digital Infrastructure

- **Description:** Empowering Africa’s cloud and digital transformation with hyperscale data center capacity serving global cloud providers.
- **Key Company:** Shinrai LLC (majority stake).
- **Impact:** Powers over 450MW of hyperscale data center capacity in Ireland and beyond.
- **Focus Areas:** Digital infrastructure, technology growth.

### 4. Real Estate & Finance

- **Description:** Financing homes and developing commercial spaces across West Africa, delivering mortgage solutions and shaping urban futures.
- **Key Companies:**
  - Lighthouse Finance & Co.
  - Lighthouse REIT.
- **Impact:** 22% of new mortgages in Nigeria.
- **Focus Areas:** Housing finance, commercial real estate development.

### 5. Natural Gas & Energy

- **Description:** Securing cleaner energy through exploration, production, and distribution of natural gas.
- **Key Company:** Pacific Gas Company Plc.
- **Focus Areas:** Gas exploration, pipelines, energy distribution.

### 6. Media Holdings

- **Description:** Strategic stakes in global information leaders promoting transparency, journalism, and informed decision-making.
- **Key Company:** Blackwell Media.
- **Focus Areas:** Media platforms across Africa.

The portfolio emphasizes sustainable infrastructure, with investments in energy transition, digital economy, and industrialization.
